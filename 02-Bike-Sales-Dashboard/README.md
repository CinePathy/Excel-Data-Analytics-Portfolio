# 🚲 Bike Sales Dashboard

An interactive **Bike Sales Dashboard built using Microsoft Excel** to analyze customer demographics, purchasing behaviour, income, occupation, commute distance, region, education, and bike purchase patterns.

The project demonstrates how customer data can be transformed into meaningful insights using **data cleaning, PivotTables, PivotCharts, KPIs, and dashboard visualization**.

## 📌 Project Overview

The objective of this project is to understand **customer purchasing behaviour** and identify patterns associated with bike purchases.

The dashboard provides an interactive view of customer characteristics and their relationship with bike purchase decisions.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

* Analyze customer purchasing behaviour.
* Identify the percentage of customers who purchased bikes.
* Analyze purchases across different age groups.
* Compare purchasing behaviour across regions.
* Analyze the relationship between commute distance and bike purchases.
* Compare bike purchases across occupations.
* Analyze customer demographics.
* Understand the influence of education and income.
* Build an interactive Excel dashboard.

---

# 📂 Dataset

The dataset contains customer information including:

The dataset contains **1,000 customer records** after preparation.

---

# 📑 Workbook Structure

The workbook contains the following sheets:

### 1. Raw Data

Contains the original customer dataset.

### 2. Cleaned_Data

Contains the cleaned and standardized dataset used for analysis.

Data preparation includes standardizing categorical values such as:

* Marital Status
* Gender
* Education
* Occupation
* Region
* Commute Distance
* Bike Purchase

### 3. Pivot Tables

Contains PivotTable-based analysis of customer purchasing behaviour.

The analysis includes:

* Purchase by age group
* Purchase rate by region
* Purchase rate by commute distance
* Purchase rate by occupation
* Purchase by gender
* Education vs bike purchase

### 4. KPI

Contains the major customer and purchasing KPIs.

### 5. Dashboard

The final interactive dashboard presenting the customer purchase analysis.

# 🔍 Key Insights

## 🚲 Overall Bike Purchase

Out of 1,000 customers:

* **481 customers purchased a bike**
* **519 customers did not purchase a bike**

Overall bike purchase rate:

### **48.1%**

---

## 👤 Purchase by Age Group

| Age Group  |  No | Yes | Total |
| ---------- | --: | --: | ----: |
| Adolescent |  71 |  39 |   110 |
| Middle Age | 318 | 383 |   701 |
| Old        | 130 |  59 |   189 |

The **Middle Age** customer group accounts for the largest number of bike purchases.

---

## 🌎 Purchase Rate by Region

| Region        | Purchase Rate |
| ------------- | ------------: |
| Europe        |        49.33% |
| North America |        43.31% |
| Pacific       |        58.85% |

The **Pacific region** has the highest bike purchase rate among the three regions.

---

## 🚗 Purchase Rate by Commute Distance

| Commute Distance | Purchase Rate |
| ---------------- | ------------: |
| 0–1 Miles        |        54.64% |
| 1–2 Miles        |        45.56% |
| 2–5 Miles        |        58.64% |
| 5–10 Miles       |        39.58% |
| 10+ Miles        |        29.73% |

The dashboard shows that purchase rates vary across commute-distance groups.

---

## 💼 Purchase Rate by Occupation

| Occupation     | Purchase Rate |
| -------------- | ------------: |
| Clerical       |        49.72% |
| Management     |        42.20% |
| Manual         |        46.22% |
| Professional   |        54.35% |
| Skilled Manual |        45.10% |

The **Professional** occupation group has the highest purchase rate among the listed occupation categories.
